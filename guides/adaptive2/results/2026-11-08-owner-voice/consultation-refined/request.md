# Feature request: recover earlier drafts

People need to recover a note they accidentally overwrote, even after closing the browser or
moving to another device. I propose keeping the last three saved versions and adding a restore
operation. I will handle choosing and previewing a version in notebook.

Please check whether you already support this or would recommend another approach. Keep existing
clients working. Explain any choices about retention, repeated saves, empty text, large drafts,
and another tab editing the same note. Recommend sensible defaults for the open choices.
