# What happened in the last 200 C2 recipe jobs

**36 of 90 Adaptive3 build histories reached Backstop.** In 38 of the 79 recorded visits, Implementation sent work to Backstop and Backstop sent it straight back to Implementation. Those 38 detours involved 17 jobs. The clearest opportunity is to let the implementer act on a failed check while it still has the candidate, diagnostics and authority to fix it. Six builds did encounter a Git outage in Backstop. Removing all six still leaves 30 of 84 builds with a Backstop visit.

This document contains the census, decision counts, case readings and one trace per job. The [themes](audit.md) explain what recurred. The [proposals](improvement-plan.md) give the suggested changes and how to judge them.

## How often the same decision came back

A visit is a distinct outer Adaptive3 state occurrence with at least one recorded task. Within each visit I counted distinct lifecycle cycle scopes by their **full occurrence IDs**, including repeat visits to an identically named node. ## How to read or reproduce the inventory

D, P, I, O and G mean Direction, Preparation, Implementation, Outcome and Integration. A number after × is the count of cycles **within that visit**. A bare letter means one. B(2) means one Backstop visit with two investigation scopes. Returning to a letter later means reentry. For a queue job the trace shows the intake and landed-stack decision states with their substeps. For a check or standalone question it shows operation order, excluding routine setup. “Last task” describes the last persisted occurrence. A cancellation may interrupt a running task. A successful job can return an unfavorable check or answer. Titles and request excerpts are abbreviated for navigation.

The job links and following reads identify the evidence. Select `--jobdb http://db.lab.c2.run:9047/c2` for the CLI. Page `c2j goals list --created-before 2026-11-23T07:10:26Z` and `c2j goals candidates GOAL` using their page tokens. Sort the retained candidate jobs by `created_at`, then ID, descending. For each job page `c2j job log JOB --json --limit 100 --output -` with its next cursor. Task numbers cited above are **one-based list positions** across concatenated pages. Use the exact task ID at that position for `job log --task ID --field /response --output -`. For a form use `/request`. Its document mapping gives the key for `job artifact JOB --ref KEY --output FILE`. Phase counts use the state directly under the adaptive3 machine and unique enclosing scope prefixes, not substring counts in prompts. Resolution comes from the root-source task’s `/resolved_commit`. Rows without that field have a dash.
