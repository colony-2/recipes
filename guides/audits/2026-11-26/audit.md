# Why reviews keep asking

The recipe opens review forms to get decisions, answer comments and report what happened. Each can put a full document back in front of the person. One Cortex form quotes “include all fixes in your change and land,” says that settles the scope, and then asks for approval of the checks for those fixes. It carries 32 files. [Cortex review](https://jobs.colony2.com/c2/jobs/3Mbh2RCm7sNR3kGMzhTuaClekmX)

I compared the two November 26 review queues at 00:08 and 19:18 UTC. Across the 20 jobs in either queue, 31 new forms opened between observations. Thirteen forms were waiting at the second cutoff. Twelve designated a primary document. Together they referenced 121 files, counting a file each time a form offered it. Six waiting forms offered only **Revise**. The direct question to the mac01 operator used **Send operator reply** and carried zero files. [Scope, counts and job-by-job findings](inventory.md)

## What was repeated

- **One screen gave opposite accounts of the same “yes.”** At 18:07 UTC the scroll job’s question said the prior “yes” covered composer tests and asked permission to change the spacing. Its attached 1P, saved at producer task 166, said that same “yes” had chosen the spacing change. The preceding question had explicitly promised to ask before changing visible behavior. The delegate had a reason to question that reading. Recipes displayed its question above the author’s uncorrected 1P. A person could read either and come away with a different understanding of what they were approving. [Scroll review](https://jobs.colony2.com/c2/jobs/3McO3PDMSul8iEKaPoEg3vla7vH) · [How the delegate’s question and author’s documents are combined](../../../recipes/adaptive3/review.yaml#L134)
- **An instruction came back as an approval request.** Cortex asked whether to combine four migration fixes with its saved browser repairs. The person said to put them together and land them. The next question asked whether the proposed checks could proceed. Full verification still mattered. No new decision about combining the fixes was identified. [Cortex DAG](https://jobs.colony2.com/c2/jobs/3Mbh2RCm7sNR3kGMzhTuaClekmX)
- **A growing list of failed tests became a growing list of permissions.** On scroll bouncing, the person selected **Take to Completion** four times. The next four plans carried 10, 12, 15 and 18 files. The delegate ran before each and chose to ask. The six focused typing checks were already passing by the third plan. The thread moved through execution-tree failures, composer tests and an eight-pixel alignment change. A separate Cortex job had already received approval for the same Original request alignment behavior. New product behavior can deserve a decision. Discovering another failing test calls first for checking what the owner already agreed to repair. - **A clarification reopened the reading assignment.** The user asked c2j what “metadata lease” meant. The reply explained that labels use a version check and that a running task uses its existing lease for permission. The form then asked for plan approval again and referenced the same six files. When the user asked how a timed retry could work while c2j was stopped, the answer kept all eight prior files, including a 10,128-byte recovery report. Both replies could have stood on their own. The c2ops parent had also sent the same four-file plan three times while answering questions about retry behavior. ## Why the document opens

Recipes constructs a clarification by copying the previous form and changing its question. That preserves its primary document, attachments and usually its approval buttons. During recovery it also writes a primary report containing the options, recent message, original request and supporting links. The report can repeat the message immediately above it. [Clarification form](../../../recipes/adaptive3/discussion.yaml#L83) · [Recovery report](../../../recipes/adaptive3/backstop-report.yaml#L33)

Cortex renders the pending review message and opens a reader for its primary document. Other files are listed as attachments. If the recipe omits the overview but still supplies files, the reader can select a primary or fall back to the first file. Older occurrences in a thread can collapse while the current occurrence still opens. I verified this in Cortex source at `0c4aebc`; the running browser bundle was not independently identified. [Pending review](https://github.com/colony-2/cortex/blob/0c4aebcfb7d1b48363581c710a1b2b9777b1de86/web/app/src/components/ReviewV2.tsx#L61) · [Document selection](https://github.com/colony-2/cortex/blob/0c4aebcfb7d1b48363581c710a1b2b9777b1de86/web/app/src/utils/reviewDocumentLocation.ts#L42) · [Thread history](https://github.com/colony-2/cortex/blob/0c4aebcfb7d1b48363581c710a1b2b9777b1de86/web/app/src/components/JobConversation.tsx#L185)
